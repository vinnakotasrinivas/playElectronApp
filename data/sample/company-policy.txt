Acme Enterprise Employee Handbook — Excerpt

1. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 local time. Laptops must use company VPN
when accessing internal systems.

2. Refund Policy for Enterprise Customers
Enterprise software subscriptions may be refunded within 30 days of the initial
purchase if less than 20% of licensed seats have been activated. After 30 days,
refunds require written approval from Finance. Professional services fees are
non-refundable once delivery has started.

3. Expense Reimbursement
Travel expenses above $75 require itemized receipts. Meals are capped at $60 per
person per day for domestic travel. Submit expenses in the portal within 14 days
of the trip end date.

4. Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per calendar year. Unused PTO may roll
over up to 5 days into the next year. Parental leave is 12 weeks for primary
caregivers.

5. Information Security
Do not share passwords. Report suspected phishing within 1 hour to security@acme.example.
Customer data must not be stored on personal devices.
